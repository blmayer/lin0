    Link mksh, toybox, and tcc with /lib/libc.so.

    musl's libc.so is the dynamic linker, so those binaries no longer
    record the ld-musl symlink name. mksh is dynamic. The x86_64 and
    x86_64-generic tarballs and their checksums match this userland.
